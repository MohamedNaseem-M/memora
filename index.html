01 // Project Identity

What Is Memora?

An ethical platform designed to help authorized family members preserve and interact with an AI representation of a deceased loved one using verified personal materials.

🏛️

Memorial Representation Platform

Authorized family members can construct a memorial profile centered on a mandatory reference photograph, supplemented by optional voice recordings, videos, messages, and documented family memories.

🔒

Controlled & Private Architecture

Designed to preserve personal history within a privacy-conscious, access-controlled repository where verification credentials remain strictly isolated from conversational memory sources.

⚠️

Critical Reality Clarification

Memora does not recreate consciousness. It provides an AI-generated interaction experience derived strictly from available and authorized materials. The platform transparently communicates this distinction at all times to prevent misunderstanding.

02 // The Problem We Address

The Challenge of Preserving Personal Legacy

Addressing memory fragmentation, degradation, and privacy risks without making unsupported psychological or medical claims.

📂

Scattered Materials

Important family memories are scattered across photographs, home videos, voice recordings, chat logs, and physical documents.

⏳

Preservation Loss

Over time, materials become difficult to preserve, access, and interpret as generational context and personal stories fade.

🖼️

Static Memorials

Existing digital memorials are often static archives that do not provide an integrated, evidence-grounded conversational experience.

🛡️

Privacy & Control

Families lack a dedicated, privacy-conscious, and controlled environment to preserve meaningful memories safely.

✨

How Memora Proposes to Address These Challenges

Memora combines a 7-stage verification framework with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and evidence output validation to organize memories, record provenance, and generate grounded conversational responses.

05 // Evidence Architecture

Three-Tier Evidence Classification

How Memora categorizes information and surfaces evidence limitations rather than inventing memories.

Level A — Direct Evidence (Primary Material)

Direct Source

Original material created or recorded directly by the deceased person (e.g., audio recordings, handwritten journals, letters, text messages, videos).

Level B — Family Testimony (Attributed Account)

Attributed Source

Memories, anecdotes, and descriptions provided by authorized family members, explicitly attributed to their source (e.g., "According to a story shared by daughter Sarah...").

Level C — AI Inference (AI-Generated Interpretation)

Explicitly Labeled Inference

AI-generated interpretation that is explicitly identified as inference. It must never be presented as a verified personal memory or a direct statement from the deceased.

💡

Grounding, Limits & Conflicting Evidence

RAG retrieves relevant evidence and output validation checks whether responses are supported. Memora does not claim hallucinations are completely eliminated. When evidence is insufficient, the system communicates that limitation. Conflicting evidence is surfaced to users rather than silently resolved.

06 // Access Control & Boundaries

Relationship-Aware Access Control

Strict ethical rules enforced at the resource level for every applicable request.

💍

Verified Adult Legal Spouses Only

Romantic or intimate interaction modes are restricted exclusively to verified adult legal spouses who have satisfied all identity, death, relationship, and adult eligibility checks.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family, Descendants & Friends Scope

Descendants and family members interact through respectful guidance, life stories, and family anecdotes. Intimate/romantic interaction modes are strictly unavailable.

🚫

Explicit Exclusions & Continuous Protection

Romantic/intimate interaction is not available to dating partners, boyfriends, girlfriends, lovers, friends, siblings, or other relationships. Authorization is enforced at the resource level on every request, and general AI safety and privacy rules remain active for all users.

12 // Research Alignment

Research Limitations & Boundary Conditions

Explicit boundaries documented in the original Memora research manuscript to preserve ethical integrity.

⚖️

Jurisdictional Variability

Verification requirements, estate laws, and death record access vary significantly across state and international jurisdictions.

🤖

RAG Hallucination Limits

RAG and output validation significantly reduce unsupported claims, but cannot guarantee that hallucinations are completely eliminated.

🎙️

Voice Similarity ≠ Identity

Acoustic voice synthesis demonstrates vocal likeness, but does not prove personal identity, authority, or personhood.

👤

Avatar Realism ≠ Consciousness

Visual photorealism and lip synchronization do not establish human consciousness, subjective awareness, or sentience.

🧠

Long-Term Psychological Effects

The long-term psychological impacts of interacting with digital legacy AI representations have not yet been clinically established.

🛡️

Security & Legal Compliance

Formal legal compliance assessments are pending, and no system can offer an absolute guarantee of complete security or privacy.
